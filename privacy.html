Make a List

Privacy

Updated November 6, 2026

Make a List stores your lists, item text, and attached photos on your device. Optional backup connections let you save copies to your own Google Drive or Dropbox account.

Information the app uses

The app stores your lists, photos, completion status, dates, and preferences in your browser’s local storage and database. Recently deleted lists remain recoverable in the app for 30 days. Changes to your browser or device storage can remove locally stored information.

When you connect Google Drive, Google provides authorization credentials. The automatic-renewal connection also checks your Google account’s verified email to restrict access to the configured account. It does not request your Google password.

Google Drive and Dropbox backups

When enabled, backups upload your list data and photos to your chosen provider. Google Drive permission is limited to files the app creates or that you specifically authorize for the app. The app does not request access to all files in your Google Drive. The backup includes recently deleted lists and may include readable text and HTML exports.

The automatic Google connection uses a backend hosted in Google Cloud to store encrypted renewal credentials and a device-session record. Your lists and photos are uploaded directly from your device to Google Drive, not stored in that backend. A short-lived Google access credential is returned to your device to perform the backup.

Use and sharing

Google account information and authorization credentials are used to connect your account and provide backup and recovery. They are not sold or used for advertising. Connected providers process the information necessary to provide their services under their own privacy policies. GitHub Pages hosts the app; Google Cloud hosts the optional automatic-connection service. Hosting providers may process technical request information for service operation and security.

Your choices and deletion

You can use the app without connecting a backup provider. Disconnecting Google Drive in the app removes this device’s automatic-connection session when the server confirms removal. You can also revoke the app’s Google access through your Google account permissions.

Deleted lists can be restored during the 30-day recovery period. Removing a list from the app does not automatically remove previously exported files, backup copies, or provider-managed file history. Manage those copies directly in Google Drive or Dropbox. Clearing the app’s browser storage removes local copies and settings; it does not delete cloud backups.

Unused automatic-connection sessions expire after 180 days. Unfinished sign-in records expire after 10 minutes. Expired records are rejected by the service and removed by the configured database cleanup process. Reconnecting creates a new device session.
